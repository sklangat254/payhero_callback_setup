NYOTA presentation payout (REAL KES 50 request)

1. Back up your existing app.py. Drag app.py and requirements.txt into your existing Render-linked GitHub backend repository. No loan_presentation.py needed.
2. In Render Environment keep all existing HashBack and Firebase settings and add:
   NYOTA_PRESENTATION_PIN = a long private authorization code
   NYOTA_ALLOWED_PHONE = the ONE M-Pesa phone number permitted for this presentation
3. Deploy. Existing Flask endpoints are unchanged.
4. Put eligibility.html and loan-result.html alongside your signup.html and savings-plan.html on the website.
5. Use the allowed phone during signup and enter your private PIN in the payout popup.
6. This sends a REAL payout request to HashBack. HashBack success means accepted, not independently confirmed delivered. The page says awaiting confirmation and redirects after acceptance.
7. One attempt per permitted phone is reserved under Firebase nyota_presentation_payouts/{254...}. Do not delete/reset this record without reconciling payment status.
8. Existing CORS allows broad origins; this is for a restricted presentation only, not a public deployment. Keep the PIN secret and never place it in frontend code.
9. Your original /hb/withdraw minimum KES 100 and balance deduction remain unchanged. The new route is separate and pays KES 50 from the HashBack wallet.
10. No Firebase Admin SDK or service account JSON is required for this package.
